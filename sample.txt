    Inside our fridge, there’s still a smidge of old and moldy ham.
    And, next to it, there’s quite a bit of dried-up, fried-up Spam.

    The bacon in the crisper bin has been there much too long.
    The sausage links have such a stink.
    Their smell is simply wrong.

    The ribs and roast are both the most disgusting ever seen.
    Yes, every piece has rancid grease and spots of bluish-green.

    We left it there without a care for weeks, or months, or years.
    Now all this rotten food we’ve got is bringing us to tears.

    I guess we should have understood it must be eaten quick.
    And if we wait till it’s too late, it might just make us sick.

    But now this meat — too old to eat, too fossilized to fork, from long before the dinosaurs —
    is all Jurassic Pork.
